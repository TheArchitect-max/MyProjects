Current-state determination

README is not the source of truth.

A current-state conclusion can be strengthened or contradicted by repository evidence outside the README. The revaluation uses multiple evidence classes and applies conservative treatment when those signals conflict.

Default-branch head & chronology

Head identity, commit timing and diffs establish what changed after the financial cutoff and prevent same-period work from being counted twice.

First-party implementation

Source modules, package structure, persistence, schemas, APIs, orchestration, security and operational surfaces establish implemented scope.

Tests & retained evidence

Test suites, exact-SHA reports, benchmark artifacts, validation bundles and machine-readable outcomes distinguish executed evidence from intended capability.

Release & transfer readiness

Version metadata, manifests, provenance, source/distribution hashes, reproducibility and handover surfaces contribute to transferability and replacement-cost analysis.

Explicit blockers

NOT_EXECUTED, blocked, synthetic-only, pending GPU, unverified physical performance and missing external qualification remain valuation constraints.

Documentation

README, release notes and technical documents are supporting evidence. They are neither automatically credited nor automatically rejected.

Five-lens architecture

Development cost does not define acquisition value.

The model retains separate lenses so build effort, market context, monetization potential, strategic utility and seller positioning are not collapsed into one arbitrary multiplier.

01

Replacement Cost

Current estimate for recreating comparable present work product. Current-head deltas are evidence-based, while the broader all-in component audit remains incomplete.

02

Market / Comparable

Modelled proxy using maturity, potential and sector context. Primary-source transactions calibrate sector confidence but are not copied into asset prices.

03

Income / Licensing

Modelled monetization proxy. A conventional income valuation still requires defensible buyer-specific cash flow or royalty evidence.

04

Probability-adjusted Strategic Value

Replacement-cost anchored opportunity value adjusted for technical completion, commercialization probability, rights/transfer reserve, time/risk and sector context.

05

Seller Asking Price

The public seller-side acquisition position remains independent of the analytical model and can deliberately sit below the economic reference.

06

Triangulation

Current weights remain replacement cost 25%, market proxy 20%, income/licensing proxy 20% and probability-adjusted strategic value 35%.

Probability architecture

Risk remains explicit.

Build progress is credited only to the extent that it reduces relevant technical, commercialization, transfer or time risk.

Technical completion probability

Research-stage 50% · Developed prototype 75% · Developed software 95%.

Commercialization probability

Specialist 25% · Moderate 40% · High 55% · Very High 70%.

Time / risk adjustment

Research-stage 70% · Developed prototype 85% · Developed software 95%.

Rights / transfer reserve

85% pending transaction-stage verification of ownership, third-party rights, licences, dependencies and transferability.

Model boundary. These probabilities and multipliers are internal modelling assumptions, not institution-prescribed rates. Asset-specific retained evidence should progressively replace generic assumptions.

Replacement-cost completeness

€62.155M is still a baseline, not a finished forensic cost audit.

The current-head review added €2.005M of evidenced development delta to the prior cost baseline. A fully loaded component audit is still required for architecture, research, QA, security, deployment, documentation, data/model assets, productization, compliance, IP qualification, programme management and avoided failed-R&D effort.

No automatic uplift. The cost baseline is not multiplied by a percentage. Already-included work must not be counted twice, and work described but not executed must not be priced as completed work.

External market evidence

Transactions calibrate context, not subject-asset price.

Thirteen primary-source transaction anchors across eight commercial sectors support the proposition that strategic software and IP can have substantial economic value. The observed companies are generally larger and more mature, so their transaction values and revenue multiples are not directly applied to these assets.
