Reassessment method

Six factors, asset by asset.

This is a portfolio-owner pricing framework—not an independent appraisal, legal opinion, fairness opinion or claim of current revenue.

01

Implementation

Current executable scope and completeness of the maintained software.

02

Validation

Documented tests, release gates, benchmarks and explicit evidence boundaries.

03

Product readiness

Packaging, deployment, security, operations, persistence, recovery and user-facing surfaces.

04

Provenance

Evidence discipline, reproducibility, data foundations and artifact integrity.

05

Commercial fit

Buyer problem, hosted-product path, product focus and plausible go-to-market model.

06

Rights posture

First-party proprietary boundaries are reviewed separately from third-party dependencies, data, models and services.
