Method

Cost, market evidence and transferability.

The current model retains recreation cost as a technical baseline and applies substantial discounts for maturity, commercial proof, transferability and liquidity. Operating-company income multiples are not applied without verified operating metrics.

What is used

  • replacement/recreation effort as a technical baseline;
  • maturity and evidence quality;
  • commercial route and specialist-market potential;
  • transferability and third-party dependency constraints;
  • market evidence for early-stage software and SaaS transaction context.

What is not assumed

  • verified portfolio ARR or profit;
  • customer retention or growth;
  • patent monopoly or FTO clearance;
  • independent appraisal status;
  • guaranteed buyer demand or sale timing.

Reference sources

External calibration remains secondary to evidence.

The model uses external material to calibrate valuation logic, not to manufacture operating metrics that the portfolio has not demonstrated.
