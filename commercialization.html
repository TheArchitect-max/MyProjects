Reassessment method

Six factors, reviewed asset by asset.

The result is a portfolio-owner pricing framework, not an independent appraisal or a claim of current revenue.

01

Implementation

Current executable scope and completeness of the maintained software.

02

Validation

Actually documented tests, release gates, benchmarks and explicit validation boundaries.

03

Product readiness

Packaging, deployment, security, operations, persistence, recovery and user-facing surfaces where applicable.

04

Provenance

Evidence discipline, reproducibility, data foundations and artifact integrity.

05

Commercial fit

Clarity of buyer/user problem, hosted-product path and plausible go-to-market model.

06

Rights & exclusivity

First-party proprietary ownership is treated separately from third-party dependency, model, data, API, standard and service rights.

Interpretation

A lower As-Is reference than recreation cost is intentional. A buyer may acquire an existing asset below estimated recreation effort while still assuming productization, diligence and market risk. First-party proprietary ownership supports transferable exclusivity. Third-party dependencies, models, datasets, APIs, standards and services remain separately licensed and can affect deployability or distribution rights where applicable.
