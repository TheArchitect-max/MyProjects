These figures are not current revenue, current enterprise value, fair market value, an independent appraisal or guaranteed transaction outcomes. They are planning scenarios derived from standardized per-project commercial-potential tiers and broad SaaS market-reference multiples.

Method

Commercial potential is modeled, not claimed as traction.

Planning anchors: Very High €2.0M ARR; High €1.0M; Moderate €0.5M; Specialist €0.25M. Actual commercialization would depend on product completion, product-market fit, customers, retention, margins and execution.

Market references: the broad 2×–7× private-SaaS scenario is benchmarked against FE International's 2026 private SaaS discussion; the 4.0× central reference uses Software Equity Group's 2Q26 SaaS M&A median-revenue reference.
