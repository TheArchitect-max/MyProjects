Commercial routes to consider.

Product extension

A software owner may assess an asset as an addition to an existing offering. Compatibility, development effort and customer demand still need verification.

Field-specific licensing

Defined usage rights may suit a buyer serving a particular industry, territory or customer group. Exclusivity and sublicensing require explicit agreement.

Research commercialisation

A specialist buyer may evaluate research-stage work for a longer development programme. Funding, feasibility and future market demand are separate questions.

Assess the business case without disclosing the implementation.

Build versus acquire

Compare the agreed transaction price, remaining work, operating costs, time to usable capability and integration costs against a buyer's own alternative. The published replacement baseline is an estimate, not proof of realised savings.

Commercial acceptance

Define the target users, intended benefit and evidence needed to support a decision. No revenue, customer base, performance improvement or market share is implied by a portfolio listing.
