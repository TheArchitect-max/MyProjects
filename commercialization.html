Valuation architecture

Cost, market and income
are different methods.

The review triangulates established valuation logic rather than converting one metric mechanically into another.

01

Cost baseline

Recreation/replacement effort remains useful for internally developed software and early-stage technology, but does not predict what a buyer will pay.

02

Market calibration

2026 SaaS and digital-asset transaction evidence is used to calibrate commercial-proof, transferability and liquidity discounts.

03

Income approach

Not applied to current portfolio pricing because verified portfolio-level ARR, profit, customer count, growth and retention are not established.

04

Maturity

Software-Validated, Verified Prototype and Research assets receive materially different conversion factors.

05

Commercial potential

Very High, High, Moderate and Specialist potential adjusts the strategic conversion without pretending future revenue already exists.

06

Route to market

B2B SaaS, specialist licensing, research licensing, web infrastructure and creator software carry different transferability profiles.

External market evidence

Multiple sources.
Consistent signal.

These sources are not direct comparables for any single asset. Together they define the market boundary used to prevent unsupported operating-company multiples from being assigned to pre-revenue IP.

WIPO

IP valuation

Cost/replacement methods are appropriate baselines for internally developed software and very early-stage technology when income forecasts and comparable transactions are weak.

ACQ

Acquire.com 2026

Closed profitable SaaS transactions center on verified operating performance; buyers anchor on profit unless scale, growth and retention justify another basis.

SEG

Software Equity Group 2Q26

SaaS M&A remained active but selective, with premiums for strategic fit, proprietary data and embedded workflows.

SC

SaaS Capital 2026

Private B2B SaaS valuation frameworks use ARR growth and net revenue retention—metrics that require real operating evidence.

FLP

Flippa liquidity boundary

Marketplace rules illustrate the material liquidity discount attached to non-revenue and concept-stage assets.

LAB

Labor-cost cross-check

BLS and Eurostat labor data support the plausibility of meaningful software recreation cost while remaining separate from transaction value.
