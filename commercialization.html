Pricing architecture

Four different numbers.
Four different meanings.

None is represented as a certified fair-market appraisal or operating-company valuation.

01

Strategic asking reference

A current seller-side target for an As-Is software/IP transfer, calibrated for maturity, potential, route to market and transferability.

02

Defensible band

A negotiation range around the strategic reference. It is intentionally broader than a single-point estimate.

03

Liquidity reference

A lower quick-sale / execution-risk layer reflecting the absence of guaranteed buyer demand and commercial proof.

04

Recreation cost

A technical replacement-effort reference. It is not the transaction price and is kept separate from market transfer value.

Method boundary

No invented revenue multiple.
