Reassessment method

Six factors, reviewed asset by asset.

The result is a portfolio owner pricing framework, not an independent appraisal or a claim of current revenue.

01

Implementation

Current executable scope and completeness of the maintained software.

02

Validation

Actually documented tests, release gates, benchmarks and explicit validation boundaries.

03

Product readiness

Packaging, deployment, security, operations, persistence, recovery and user-facing surfaces where applicable.

04

Provenance

Evidence discipline, reproducibility, data foundations and artifact integrity.

05

Commercial fit

Clarity of buyer/user problem, hosted-product path and plausible go-to-market model.

06

Rights & exclusivity

Transfer value is discounted where current repository licensing makes the codebase non-exclusive.

Interpretation

A lower As-Is reference than recreation cost is intentional. A buyer may acquire an existing asset below the estimated cost of recreating comparable software while still assuming productization, diligence and market risk. Non-exclusive open-source licensing is treated as a material discount to transferable exclusivity even when the engineering replacement effort remains substantial.
