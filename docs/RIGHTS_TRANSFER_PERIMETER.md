# Rights & Transfer Perimeter Standard

Basis date: 2026-10-07

## What is being offered

For each asset, the transaction perimeter is the seller-owned first-party intellectual-property rights that are legally transferable or licensable, together with the repository, documentation, tests, build/release material and other deliverables expressly listed in the definitive transaction documents.

## What is not automatically transferred as seller-owned IP

- open-source software;
- third-party libraries and frameworks;
- third-party datasets or models;
- external APIs and hosted services;
- third-party media, fonts or other content;
- any right owned by another party.

These items remain subject to their own licences and agreements.

## Buyer-relevant rights questions

The seller-side dossier must make it possible to answer:

1. What first-party asset/right is offered?
2. What evidence supports the seller's control of that asset?
3. Are any external human co-owners known or disclosed?
4. What third-party IP is incorporated or required?
5. What obligations or transfer restrictions apply?
6. What exact source, documentation and delivery artifacts are included?
7. What is explicitly excluded?
8. What rights representation/warranty will appear in the definitive agreement?

The seller does not need to publish the internal development method to answer these questions.
