# Seller Evidence & Transfer Readiness Standard

**Current revision:** 2026-10-12T09:21:42Z. The 104 active project profiles each disclose five recorded assessment axes, the handover decision and project-specific outstanding conditions. Current labels: **0 READY, 94 READY_WITH_CONDITIONS, 10 NOT_READY**.

| Label | Transfer grade | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| READY | T4 | Content-reviewed, release-specific delivery; reconciled component, rights, security and provenance evidence; no unresolved seller-side handover blockers. |
| READY_WITH_CONDITIONS | T2–T3 | A bounded source/documentation delivery basis exists, with explicit remaining agreement, qualification or acceptance conditions. |
| NOT_READY | T0–T1 | Essential implementation or handover evidence is incomplete, or an expressly reported transfer blocker remains open. |

The transfer grade determines the label. Technical, evidence, rights and commercial grades provide separate context. Asking price is not a readiness criterion. No label establishes production suitability, scientific validity, regulatory approval or buyer acceptance.

| Axis | Recorded basis |
|---|---|
| Technical scope | Developed software, developed prototype, research or existing definition documents. Future objectives are separate. |
| Evidence | E0: none identified; E1: tests/structured evidence; E2: tests plus validation; E3: additional build/release/provenance evidence; E4: version-bound external execution or independent validation evidence. |
| Rights | R0: licence evidence not identified; R1: identified licence basis; R2: additional dependency/third-party disclosure; R3: component/locked-dependency and provenance evidence; R4: independent legal title evidence. Missing licence evidence does not establish absence of first-party ownership. |
| Commercial | C0: no reference; C1: commercial framing; C2: seller ask and reproducible analytical model; C3: attributable transactions/income evidence; C4: independent professional valuation. |
| Transfer | T0: no implementation; T1: essential handover evidence incomplete; T2: bounded source, documentation, tests and rights basis; T3: additional validation/build/dependency evidence; T4: substantively verified complete delivery and closed seller blockers. |

READY requires substantive content verification. An inventory, filename, generator or supplied PASS declaration is not proof that a release was tested or delivered. Negative readiness reports take precedence over the existence of a dossier. A technical software PASS does not close a separately blocked commercial/rights gate.

Main and unmerged development results are version-specific. Synthetic fixtures do not establish real-world performance; small examples do not prove production scale; integrity digests do not certify authenticity or title.
