# Trade-Secret Classification Standard

Basis date: 2026-10-07

## Core distinction

This portfolio distinguishes three different states:

1. **Deleted / no longer retained information** — not a trade secret because the information is not retained as an asset.
2. **Public information** — not treated as secret information in this portfolio classification.
3. **Surviving non-public know-how** — confidential and eligible for trade-secret treatment only where the applicable legal criteria are met and reasonable secrecy measures are maintained.

The deleted multi-AI conversational histories are therefore classified as:

`HISTORICAL_DEVELOPMENT_PATH = IRRECOVERABLE_FROM_RETAINED_EVIDENCE`

and **not** as trade-secret content.

## Potential protected categories

Where they still exist and remain non-public:

- development methods;
- prompt / iteration strategies;
- unpublished architecture rationale;
- calibration and tuning know-how;
- private validation artifacts;
- unpublished research notes;
- private commercial strategy and roadmap;
- transaction-confidential information;
- non-public transfer and deployment know-how.

## Evidence rule

A trade-secret classification must refer to information that still exists. Absence of history is not evidence of a secret.

## Disclosure rule

Public catalogue pages expose only screening-level metadata. Confidential technical or commercial material should be disclosed only through a controlled transaction channel and only to the extent required.
