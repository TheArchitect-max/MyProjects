# Seller Rights & Transfer Declaration

Basis date: 2026-10-07

## Purpose

This declaration addresses the rights position relevant to acquisition or licensing. It does not disclose, and does not require disclosure of, the internal methods by which the projects were created.

## Seller declaration

The seller declares, to the seller's knowledge and subject to the disclosed third-party rights and applicable law:

- the seller controls the seller-owned first-party rights offered for sale or licensing;
- no external human co-owner of those first-party rights is known or disclosed;
- third-party software, libraries, frameworks, datasets, models, media, APIs and other third-party intellectual property are not represented as seller-owned;
- third-party material remains governed by its own licence or contract;
- a transaction transfers or licenses only the seller-owned rights and specifically identified deliverables within the definitive agreement;
- development methods, internal workflows, prompts, research process and other non-public know-how are outside the public transaction disclosure unless specifically included in a definitive transaction.

## Evidence

The seller-side evidence set may include:

- current repository and release baselines;
- Git object identities and release hashes;
- repository licence/copyright material;
- dependency manifests and lockfiles;
- SBOM/SPDX/CycloneDX material where available;
- THIRD_PARTY / NOTICE / attribution material;
- rights, transfer and delivery manifests;
- seller representations in the definitive agreement.

## Boundary

This declaration is seller-side evidence. It is not an independent legal chain-of-title opinion, freedom-to-operate opinion or warranty beyond the terms of a signed definitive agreement. Any transaction should use an agreed disclosure schedule and representations/warranties appropriate to its scope.
