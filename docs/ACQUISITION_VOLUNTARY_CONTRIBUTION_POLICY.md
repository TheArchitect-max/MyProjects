# Acquisition & Voluntary Contribution Policy

Published: 2026-10-10T09:36:25Z

Each eligible project in the THEARCHITECT_MAX portfolio may provide two completely separate routes: Acquire the Complete Project — As Is; and Make a Voluntary Bitcoin Contribution.
These routes are independent. A voluntary contribution does not purchase, reserve or create rights in a project. An acquisition is not a donation or voluntary contribution.

# 1. Acquire the Complete Project — AS IS

## Available for Acquisition

Where a project is marked Available for Acquisition, the complete defined project asset may be acquired AS IS.

The acquisition concerns the project in its actual documented state and agreed transfer scope at the transaction date.

The project is not represented as automatically:

- complete against every original or future objective;
- production-ready;
- commercially deployed;
- independently certified;
- regulator-approved;
- independently valued;
- free from further engineering requirements;
- free from third-party dependencies or obligations;
- suitable for every intended use.

## Existing Asset, Defined Scope

The buyer is acquiring the defined existing asset, not a promise that every possible development objective will subsequently be completed.

## AS IS Principle

AS IS means that the buyer evaluates and acquires the project based on its existing:

- source code;
- documentation;
- current implementation;
- version state;
- retained evidence;
- validation records;
- architecture;
- first-party intellectual property;
- project-specific assets;
- identified third-party dependencies;
- known limitations;
- outstanding conditions;
- transfer documentation.

## Included and Excluded Materials

Only materials and rights expressly included in the definitive transaction are transferred.

Third-party software, libraries, models, datasets, APIs, standards, media, infrastructure services and other external material remain subject to their respective rights, licences and terms.

## Published Asking Reference

Where shown, the published amount is a seller-side asking reference for acquisition discussions. It is not:

- an independent valuation;
- a certified appraisal;
- a guaranteed market value;
- a securities valuation;
- an investment return forecast;
- evidence of a completed comparable transaction.

## Final Price

The final purchase price and transfer perimeter are determined by the definitive agreement between seller and buyer.

## Due Diligence

Before completing an acquisition, the prospective buyer is expected to evaluate the project and the proposed transfer scope.

Additional controlled information may be made available where appropriate for legitimate acquisition due diligence.

The buyer remains responsible for determining whether the project is suitable for the buyer’s intended use.

## Definitive Agreement

A project is transferred only under a definitive written agreement defining, as applicable:

- the project identity;
- agreed purchase price;
- transferred first-party intellectual-property rights;
- included source and documentation;
- included evidence and artifacts;
- excluded materials;
- third-party dependencies and obligations;
- delivery method;
- confidentiality requirements;
- representations and warranties;
- acceptance conditions;
- applicable law and other transaction terms.

## No Transfer Through Publication

Information published on the portfolio website does not itself transfer ownership or intellectual-property rights.

## GitHub Pages Transaction Boundary

The public GitHub Pages website functions as an informational project and IP portfolio.

No purchase, payment, investment or transfer of intellectual property is completed through GitHub Pages itself.

GitHub Pages does not operate the contractual transfer process, user accounts, transaction database, escrow, billing or payment settlement for acquisition transactions.

A prospective buyer may use the website to identify the project and initiate acquisition discussions. The definitive commercial transaction is completed separately from GitHub Pages.

## No Financial Return

No financial return is promised or offered.

## No Development Obligation

A voluntary contribution does not obligate THEARCHITECT_MAX to:

- continue a particular project;
- implement a requested feature;
- reach a specified milestone;
- maintain a particular development schedule;
- make a project public;
- release source code;
- commercialise the project;
- provide contributor benefits;
- provide services to the contributor.

## Existing Rights Holder

The project remains under the control of its existing rights holder unless and until a separate acquisition or other written agreement provides otherwise.

## Project Reference

Where a contributor intends a contribution to express support for a particular project, the relevant project identifier may be referenced, for example: TA-IP-022 — Distributed AI Resource Fabric.

Such a reference is administrative only. It does not create ownership, control, contractual development rights or any other economic interest in that project.

## Tax and Regulatory Responsibility

Contributors remain responsible for their own tax, accounting, regulatory and reporting obligations. The recipient remains responsible for applicable obligations relating to received contributions.

A voluntary contribution should not be interpreted as an investment or commercial purchase merely because a project also has a separately published acquisition price.

# Strict Separation of Both Routes

## Acquisition

Purpose: acquire the defined project asset.

Consideration: agreed purchase price.

Result: rights and materials defined by the definitive transfer agreement.

## Voluntary Contribution

Purpose: voluntarily support independent work.

Consideration: none.

Result: no ownership, repayment, financial return, IP rights or contractual development entitlement.
