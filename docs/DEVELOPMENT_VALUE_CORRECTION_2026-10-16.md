## Cost and asking rules

Role assumptions retain the existing method: EUR 18,000 senior, EUR 22,000 specialist and EUR 16,000 validation/provenance per 160-hour month, plus 15% separate coordination/tooling. Assumed work-package effort is professional judgement for reproducing the described existing functionality; it is not measured labour. AI assistance, reusable alternatives, acceptance depth and the buyer's skills can materially lower actual reproduction costs.

Each new ask = (retained base + adopted increment) x previous asset-specific ask/base proportion, rounded to EUR 500. Existing asset-specific lower/upper negotiation proportions are retained and rounded to EUR 500. No new market-demand, revenue, scientific-success or strategic multiplier is added. All existing internal model factors remain unchanged.

Illustrative lower-effort budgets use one-quarter of the assumed hours at the Knab mean of EUR 94/hour plus the same allowance; upper-effort budgets use 1.5 times the role budget. They are sensitivity alternatives, not contractor quotes, confidence intervals or established bounds.

[WIPO cost-method guidance](https://www.wipo.int/web-publications/intellectual-property-valuation-basics-for-technology-transfer-professionals/en/4-the-cost-method.html) supports examining reproduction/replacement cost while distinguishing cost from buyer value. [Knab 2026](https://bieb.knab.nl/ondernemen/wat-verdient-een-software-developer-zzp-bekijk-uurtarief-en-winst) supplies Dutch rate context; it does not validate these hours or role premiums. The existing Upwork source remains limited to its primary-publisher search result because its pages returned HTTP 403. None of these publishers appraises the projects.
