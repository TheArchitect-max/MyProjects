Maturity terminology

Three labels.
Three bounded claims.

These are portfolio maturity classifications based on repository evidence. They are not regulatory approvals, scientific endorsements or transaction warranties.

Software-Validated · 36Internal scope passed

Repository-maintained executable checks passed for the stated software behavior. Field performance and external certification remain separate.

Verified Prototype · 24Implemented and bounded

A real prototype and validation architecture are present, while broader software, operational or domain validation remains open.

Research · 4Hypothesis remains open

A repository-backed research implementation exists, but its core scientific, experimental or product claim is unresolved.

Repository verifiedExistence, not correctness

The private repository and recorded first-party posture were observed through the authorized account.

Standards terminology

Reference is not
certification.

A standards name can only support the scope actually mapped and tested. The portfolio does not use standards vocabulary as a substitute for evidence.

Standards-referencedTechnical input

A named standard, benchmark or authoritative method informs the design or evidence model. Conformity is not implied.

Standards-alignedMapped and tested

Requires an explicit requirements/control mapping, implemented controls and test evidence for a defined scope.

Independently assessedExternal review

Requires an identified competent external reviewer, method, evidence and dated scope.

CertifiedCertificate required

Requires a current certificate, issuing body, certified scope and validity period. No blanket portfolio certification is claimed.

Current boundary

Named sources such as NIST SP 800-63B-4, BIPM/NIST metrology references, NASA C-MAPSS and the NRL Plasma Formulary are disclosed as standards, benchmark or authoritative-reference inputs where applicable. Their use does not mean NIST, BIPM, NASA, NRL, ISO, IEC or another body has certified the asset.

Evidence hierarchy

Promotion requires
stronger proof.

An asset may advance only when the evidence required by the next layer exists for the exact version and stated scope.
