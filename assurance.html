Terminology

Reference is not certification.

No blanket portfolio certification is claimed. Internal tests, scientific validation, field validation, standards alignment and independent certification are different evidence states.

Software-Validated

Repository evidence indicates an executable software baseline with internal validation/test evidence. This label does not imply independent certification, production deployment or real-world performance.

Verified Prototype

An executable implementation and structured verification surfaces are present, but the audit does not establish the stronger release baseline used for Software-Validated status.

Research

The asset is principally an R&D platform, contains incomplete empirical gates, or deliberately limits claims to bounded experiments and reproducible research evidence.

Independent certification

Not inferred from internal tests, standards references, synthetic data, code review or repository state. A separate accredited/independent process is required where applicable.

Assurance boundary. Repository existence and internal validation do not establish scientific or clinical validity, legal chain-of-title, regulatory compliance, patent freedom-to-operate, cybersecurity certification, customer acceptance or commercial success. Third-party software, datasets, standards, APIs and services retain their own rights and terms.
