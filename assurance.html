Assurance model

Six evidence layers.

No blanket portfolio certification is claimed. The labels below describe what the site may and may not infer from repository evidence.

01

Repository evidence

Existence, privacy state, current product identity and observable first-party licensing boundaries.

02

Software validation

Deterministic or reproducible internal tests, validators, fixtures, benchmarks or release evidence where present.

03

Scientific / field evidence

Domain claims require appropriate datasets, protocols, external measurement or subject-matter validation. Code tests alone do not establish scientific truth.

04

Standards posture

Use of a standard, schema or interoperability format is not equivalent to standards certification or conformity assessment.

05

Independent certification

Only an authorized independent body can confer a certification within its defined scope. The portfolio currently makes no blanket certification claim.

06

Legal / transaction diligence

Copyright title, trademark, patent/FTO, third-party rights, privacy obligations and transaction transferability require transaction-specific review.
