Public information, protected know-how

Disclosure & evidence boundaries.

A commercial profile should explain the opportunity while keeping implementation knowledge outside the public presentation. Access to further information is considered separately for each asset.

What belongs at each stage.

StageAppropriate informationBoundary
Public discoveryName, TA-IP reference, purpose, sector, seller-assessed stage, asking reference and possible transaction routes.No implementation instructions, proprietary methods or confidential supporting material.
Qualified discussionIntended use, commercial fit, proposed rights and an agreed scope for further review.A discussion or enquiry does not grant access or usage rights.
Confidential diligenceOnly the evidence needed for the agreed review, where available and permitted.Separate confidentiality terms, authorised recipients and asset-specific disclosure approval.
Definitive transactionThe precisely identified rights and deliverables stated in signed agreements.Access, use, transfer and any retention obligations follow those agreements.

This is the proposed disclosure process. The public website is not a confidential data room and does not itself establish access controls or a confidentiality agreement.

Evidence has different meanings.

Public portfolio statement

A seller-supplied description or commercial classification. It helps initial screening and remains subject to verification.

Seller-supplied evidence

Material supporting a defined claim, if available. Scope, date, limitations and provenance should accompany it.

Reproduced result

A result repeated under specified conditions. It supports only the evaluated claim and environment.

Independent assessment

Review by an identified independent party within an agreed scope. No such portfolio-wide certification is claimed here.

Know-how stays outside the public page.

Restricted material

Asset source code, implementation designs, proprietary algorithms, model assets, private datasets, experimental protocols, detailed test artefacts and access credentials are outside the intended public presentation.

Rights remain distinct

A first-party asset may depend on separately licensed components or data. Confidentiality, ownership, permission to use and permission to transfer are separate diligence questions.

Trade-secret protection is conditional. WIPO describes secrecy, commercial value from secrecy and reasonable protective steps as key considerations. A private label or website notice alone does not establish protection. WIPO trade-secret guidance.

Claims must follow the evidence.

No patent grant, freedom to operate, security certification, regulatory approval, clinical effectiveness or scientific breakthrough is established by a listing. Proposed uses and buyer benefits are hypotheses for qualification.

Public enquiries must contain only the asset reference and a non-confidential indication of interest. Do not post personal contact details, source material or transaction-sensitive information. Arrange an appropriate private channel before exchanging further details.
