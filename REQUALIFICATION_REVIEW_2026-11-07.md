## Evaluation protocol (per asset; re-run after material changes)

1. Pin the repository identity, `main` revision, and any unmerged development candidate separately.
2. Establish what is *implemented* rather than described as an objective.
3. Examine actual test/evidence references and identify whether the evidence is source-specific, manually inspected, independently re-executed, or merely described.
4. Classify development maturity and production/readiness independently; recorded PASS never transfers to changed source.
5. Review third-party licence/model/content terms, patent and source ownership exposure; no implied title or resale entitlement.
6. Record product differentiation, target buyer, dependency/hosting costs and genuine reproducibility gaps.
7. Reconsider seller asking references against substantiated capability and current market evidence; never equate asking price with valuation.
8. Reconcile the product brief, website dossier, identity register, evaluation matrix and release gates. On FAIL, NOT_RUN or NOT_VERIFIED, preserve HOLD.
