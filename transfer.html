Transaction sequence

Evidence package before transfer.

A buyer should underwrite the exact repository state, asset scope, third-party obligations and transfer rights rather than relying on a portfolio card or price label.

1. Technical diligence

Freeze exact repository commits, inventory source and generated artifacts, reproduce available tests, validate build instructions and identify incomplete migrations or external runtime dependencies.

2. IP and third-party diligence

Review first-party authorship, historical license notices, third-party code/models/data, trademarks, patents/FTO and any terms that affect assignment, hosting or resale.

3. Commercial underwriting

Reassess current product scope, target users, hosting requirements, operating costs, security obligations and realistic route to market. Portfolio asking references are not substitutes for buyer underwriting.

4. Controlled handover

Define exactly what transfers: repository state, documentation, domains where applicable, signing material if appropriate, operational knowledge and any exclusions. Secrets and credentials require a separate controlled process.
