As-Is transfer framework

A software asset can be acquired before it becomes a finished SaaS business.

Each listed project is presented as a distinct owner-held software/IP asset with an individual As-Is price. A potential transfer is structured around the asset in its then-current development state and the seller-held rights applicable to that project.

Transfer pathway

Four commercial steps.

The public showroom creates the starting point; project-specific diligence and definitive terms follow privately.

01

Select

Identify the asset, its asking price, maturity, GitHub existence evidence, data/evidence foundation where applicable, and intended commercial direction.

02

Review

Confirm the proposed transaction scope, ownership/licensing posture and project-specific diligence requirements.

03

Agree

Define the exact seller-held rights, project materials, price and transfer terms in a definitive agreement.

04

Transfer

Complete the agreed handover of the software/IP asset and associated project materials.
