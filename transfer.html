Transfer pathway

Four controlled steps.

The workflow separates public selection from authorized repository review, commercial agreement and final handover.

01

Select

Compare purpose, maturity, strategic ask, negotiation band, liquidity reference, recreation cost, GitHub proof and evidence foundations.

02

Review

Under authorized diligence, confirm repository scope, authorship and chain-of-title evidence, technical state, commercial evidence and third-party obligations.

03

Agree

Define first-party rights and materials, negotiated price and settlement currency, warranties if any, exclusions, transition scope and terms.

04

Transfer

Complete the agreed software/IP handover without purporting to transfer third-party rights beyond their applicable terms.
