Transaction preparation

Buyer diligence & transfer scope.

Agree what is being considered, how it will be assessed and what would be delivered. The following framework describes negotiation topics; it is not a representation that every asset already has a complete transfer package.

Define the transaction unit.

StructureDiscussion pointDefine explicitly
Asset acquisitionAssignment of agreed rights in a named asset.Included material, retained rights, third-party exclusions and transfer conditions.
Exclusive licenceExclusive permission within an agreed scope.Field of use, territory, duration, retained uses and any performance conditions.
Non-exclusive licencePermission while other licensing may remain possible.Permitted uses, modification rights, redistribution and sublicensing.
Multiple named assetsA negotiated schedule of separately identified assets.Individual rights, values, dependencies and acceptance requirements.

Licensing and assignment differ in the rights involved; the scope must be agreed in the applicable documentation. European Union guidance on licensing and selling IP.

Six questions before terms.

What is included?

Identify the asset, version or agreed state, proposed rights and precise deliverable schedule. Family membership adds no automatic entitlement.

What can be transferred?

Review ownership, contribution history, restrictions and any permissions needed for third-party material.

What supports the claims?

Request evidence for the intended use and identify untested assumptions, limitations and remaining work.

What will it cost to use?

Consider hosting, computing, third-party charges, integration, staffing and ongoing operation. Acquisition does not eliminate operating costs.

Who will operate it?

Allocate implementation, maintenance, security, updates and compliance responsibilities. Services are not automatically included.

What completes delivery?

Define the acceptance process, delivery evidence, any agreed review period and treatment of exceptions.

Possible transfer-package categories.

Commercial and rights record

An agreed asset schedule, rights description, exclusions, provenance summary and third-party restrictions. Availability and completeness are established during diligence.

Agreed delivery material

The relevant work product, documentation and evaluation material only to the extent expressly included and legally transferable. Detailed inventories remain confidential.

Separate commercial terms. Price, currency, payment timing, delivery conditions, royalties, reporting, renewal, termination and dispute arrangements require agreement. Neither escrow nor a particular payment method is offered by this page.

Frequently asked.

Does a listing mean the asset is ready for production?

No. The stage is a seller assessment. Suitability for a proposed deployment must be verified separately.

Does buying an asset include a hosted service or ongoing support?

No automatic service commitment is included. Hosting, implementation, maintenance and support must be expressly agreed if required.

Can several assets be acquired together?

Yes, subject to negotiation. Each asset and its rights must be identified separately; a classification family is not a bundled product.

Can I inspect confidential material immediately?

No. First identify the asset and intended transaction. Any further access requires separate qualification and agreed confidentiality arrangements.

Are the values independently certified?

No. The published amounts are seller asking references, cost estimates and preliminary economic models. They are not audited appraisals.
