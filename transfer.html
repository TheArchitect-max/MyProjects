As-Is diligence sequence

What a serious transfer should bind.

01Freeze subject

Identify repository, branch/commit, release files and cryptographic fingerprints.

02Inventory rights

Separate original first-party material from third-party code, datasets, models, APIs, standards and trademarks.

03Verify evidence

Re-run relevant internal validation and preserve logs, manifests, SBOMs and attestations where available.

04Define exclusions

Document secrets, accounts, hosted services, personal data, credentials and non-transferable third-party components.

05Legal diligence

Address chain-of-title, contractual restrictions, privacy, trademark and patent/FTO issues proportionate to the transaction.

06Execute transfer

Use a written agreement defining the exact assets, warranties, liabilities, consideration and post-transfer obligations.

Third-party rights remain third-party rights.

A proprietary first-party repository boundary does not erase dependency licenses, model terms, dataset rights, API terms, standards copyrights or trademarks. Any transaction must preserve those external rights and conditions.
