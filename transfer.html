As-Is transfer framework · 03 Sep 2026

Acquire the asset in its current evidence state.

Each listed project is backed by a verified private GitHub repository. The current product tree has also been reviewed for a proprietary/all-rights-reserved first-party license boundary. That repository evidence is a diligence starting point—not independent legal chain-of-title certification.

Transfer pathway

Four commercial steps.

The public showroom is the selection layer; repository-level technical and rights diligence and definitive terms follow privately.

01

Select

Review purpose, reassessed maturity, asking reference, recreation reference, GitHub proof and available data/evidence foundation.

02

Review

Under authorized diligence, confirm exact repository scope, authorship/chain-of-title evidence, third-party licenses and terms, dependency/data obligations and project-specific technical evidence.

03

Agree

Define the exact first-party rights and materials transferred, price, warranties if any, third-party exclusions, transition scope and handover terms in a definitive agreement.

04

Transfer

Complete the agreed handover of the software/IP asset and associated materials within the negotiated scope, without purporting to transfer third-party rights beyond their terms.

What the showroom establishes

A disciplined acquisition starting point.

The public layer provides enough information to compare assets without exposing private repository identity, source code, branch history, internal architecture or transaction-only diligence materials.

Rights boundary

Current repository licenses reserve first-party rights. Third-party libraries, models, datasets, APIs, standards, services and other external materials remain governed by their own terms. Chain-of-title, trademark, patent/FTO and transaction-specific transferability require separate diligence.
