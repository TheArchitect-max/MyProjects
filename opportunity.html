Valuation methodology

Six institutional reference frameworks inform the seller-side calculations.

The individual commercial references were calculated by ChatGPT for the portfolio owner using a reproducible seller-side IP valuation framework. The reference set used was WIPO, International Valuation Standards / IVSC, OECD guidance on intangibles and value creation, and institutional IP guidance associated with JPO, USPTO and EPO.

01

Cost approach

Recreation and replacement effort are used as a cost-based reference, adjusted for asset scope, maturity and reusable work product.

02

Market approach

Commercial positioning is calibrated against comparable technology categories, transferability, buyer utility and plausible transaction context where defensible comparables are available.

03

Income & strategic utility

Potential economic benefit, licensing or integration utility, development risk and the ability of a buyer to accelerate deployment inform the seller-side range where forward economics can be reasonably considered.

04

IP-specific factors

Identifiability, maturity, provenance, evidence, rights perimeter, dependencies, transferability, useful life, value-creation contribution and commercialization readiness are considered at asset level.

Methodology boundary. “Aligned with” or “informed by” these frameworks does not mean WIPO, IVSC, OECD, JPO, USPTO or EPO reviewed, certified, endorsed or approved any specific portfolio figure. The resulting amounts remain calculated indicative seller-side valuations for professional discussion.

Transaction structures

01

Single asset

02

Multiple named assets

Two or more standalone assets negotiated together, with each asset explicitly listed and separately diligenced.

03

Licence

Negotiated use rights for a defined asset without transferring unrelated portfolio assets.

04

Broader portfolio transaction

A larger acquisition only where the counterparty explicitly identifies and diligences the included assets.
