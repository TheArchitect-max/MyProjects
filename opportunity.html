Commercial opportunity

79 separate assets.
One portfolio.

The portfolio contains 79 standalone IP assets organised into 12 classification families. Each project remains independently identifiable, independently diligenced and independently transferable in principle. Asking price and economic potential are deliberately treated as different concepts.

Where value can emerge

Strategic fit is assessed asset by asset.

Existing customers, channels, domain capability, infrastructure and implementation capacity can materially change the economics of an individual software or research asset.

01

Product acceleration

Acquire or license a defined asset instead of rebuilding an equivalent capability from zero.

02

Distribution leverage

A buyer with customers and channels may commercialise a developed asset faster than a standalone owner.

03

R&D replacement

An acquirer may avoid substantial internal recreation cost, failed experimentation and development delay by acquiring an existing asset.

04

Technology transfer

Selected software capability or implementation assets may be transferred or licensed within an asset-specific negotiated perimeter.

Reference architecture

The methodology now extends beyond pure code-development cost.

Core valuation standards are combined with intangible-asset recognition, value-creation, IP-management and institutional commercialization guidance. The published numbers remain seller-side references; the named institutions have not certified individual figures.

WIPO

Cost, market, income, real-options and Monte Carlo valuation concepts for IP and technology transfer.

IVS / IVSC

Valuation framework, bases, approaches, data, models, reporting and IVS 210 Intangible Assets.

RICS

Professional standard on valuation of intellectual-property rights, including legal, functional and economic characteristics.

OECD

Intangibles and DEMPE/value-creation analysis as a check on economic contribution and control.

IFRS IAS 38

Identifiability, separability, reliable measurement and future-economic-benefit concepts for intangible assets including software.

ISO 56005

Systematic IP-management guidance supporting innovation and commercialization readiness.

EPO / JPO

Supplementary institutional IP valuation and patent-management guidance.

USPTO

Supplementary IP valuation, technology-transfer, commercialization and economic-context resources.

Transaction structures

The asset remains the transaction unit.

Families can assist discovery, but definitive transaction documents should identify every included project separately.

01

Single asset

One defined standalone technology or software IP asset.

02

Multiple named assets

Two or more standalone assets negotiated together, with each asset explicitly listed and separately diligenced.

03

Licence

Negotiated use rights for a defined asset without transferring unrelated portfolio assets.

04

Broader portfolio transaction

A larger acquisition only where the counterparty explicitly identifies and diligences the included assets.

Additional commercial pathways

Match capability to a buyer's existing business.

Product extension, field-specific licensing, private organisational use and research commercialisation are possible directions. Their viability depends on the named asset, permitted rights and buyer execution.

What a buyer may contribute

Customer relationships, sector knowledge, distribution, product management and operating capacity may change the business case. No customer contracts or market access are implied to be included with an asset.

What a buyer must still assess

Remaining development, recurring costs, evidence quality, third-party permissions and adoption risk remain part of the acquisition decision. Avoid counting the same benefit as both a cost saving and incremental revenue.
