Commercial opportunity

Find a relevant capability.
Define the buyer benefit.

The potential opportunity lies in reusable software, research tooling and specialist workflows. Value depends on the work actually transferable and its usefulness to a specific buyer.

Three possible transaction rationales.

Product integration

Evaluate whether an implemented capability can extend an existing product with less effort than an equivalent rebuild.

Research enablement

Assess whether reproducible tools, simulations or evidence workflows support a defined research programme.

Specialist workflow

Test a domain-focused capability with intended users and establish the cost and benefit of operating it.

These are commercial hypotheses, not verified savings, sales prospects or promised outcomes. Integration, maintenance, infrastructure and remaining validation can materially affect buyer value.

One project, one defined perimeter.

Related work can create useful choices, but shared subject matter does not merge rights. A multi-project transaction needs an explicit list of included work, dependency and overlap review, and a buyer-specific rationale.
