Commercial drivers

What can create buyer-specific value.

The asking reference is a basis for discussion, not a substitute for buyer underwriting.

01

Existing distribution

A buyer with customers, channels or enterprise relationships may commercialise selected assets faster.

02

Portfolio bundling

Related assets may be combined into broader product suites, vertical offerings or licensing packages.

03

Strategic integration

Selected assets may complement an acquirer's existing product portfolio, services or market position.

04

Licensing optionality

Field-of-use, territory, term or exclusivity structures can create value without a full acquisition.

05

Productisation

Developed prototypes may offer upside where a buyer can add packaging, distribution and market validation.

06

Specialist buyer fit

Research and specialist assets may be more valuable to targeted strategic buyers than to broad-market purchasers.
