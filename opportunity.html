Commercial drivers

What can create buyer-specific upside.

The portfolio asking reference is not a substitute for buyer underwriting. Strategic value can differ materially by acquirer and transaction perimeter.

01

Existing distribution

A buyer with customers, sales channels or enterprise relationships may commercialise selected assets faster than a standalone owner.

02

Portfolio bundling

Related assets may be combined into broader product suites, vertical offerings or licensing packages.

03

Strategic integration

Selected assets may complement an acquirer's existing product stack, services or intellectual-property position.

04

Licensing optionality

Field-of-use, territory or exclusivity structures can create commercial value without requiring a full portfolio sale.

05

Productisation

Developed prototypes may offer asymmetric upside where a buyer can add distribution, packaging and market validation.

06

Specialist markets

Research and specialist assets may be more valuable to targeted strategic buyers than to broad-market purchasers.
