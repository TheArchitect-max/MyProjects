Portfolio structure

Families classify. Assets transact.

Retired predecessors are excluded from active totals. Every remaining active project is its own IP asset; a classification family exists only to group related subject matter for browsing and comparison.

01

Single asset

One standalone technology or software asset with its own TA-IP reference and defined commercial perimeter.

02

Multiple assets

Two or more standalone assets may be negotiated together only when every included asset is explicitly identified.

03

Licence

Negotiated rights apply to the named asset or assets and do not automatically extend to other members of the same classification family.

04

Portfolio transaction

A broader transaction remains possible, but each included IP asset retains its own identity and diligence perimeter.
