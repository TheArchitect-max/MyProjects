Claim discipline

Three boundaries stay explicit.

A portfolio can be technically serious without overstating what its repository evidence proves. This site therefore separates internal software validation, external assurance and legal/commercial diligence.

Software validation

Internal deterministic tests, release evidence, reproducibility controls and bounded benchmark results can support a software maturity classification. They do not automatically establish scientific, clinical, field or regulatory validation.

Independent certification

No blanket third-party certification claim is made across the portfolio. Standards references and standards alignment remain distinct from accreditation or formal certification.

IP & transfer

Private-repository control and proprietary first-party notices are diligence inputs, not a substitute for transaction-specific chain-of-title, third-party rights, trademark, patent/FTO, privacy, export or regulatory review.
