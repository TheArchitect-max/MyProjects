Harm Booij

THEARCHITECT_MAX began as an independent hobbyist software-building effort in October 2025 and developed into a deliberate portfolio concept: build multiple privately held software assets, improve each on its own roadmap, and create a common public and commercial Hub around the strongest ventures.

The projects are not represented as having a team, institutional funding, established customers or production SaaS revenue unless that becomes separately evidenced. The purpose of this site is to present the projects professionally without exposing the proprietary implementation behind them.
