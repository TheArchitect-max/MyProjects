Important notice

Standalone asset
boundaries matter.

This website is a commercial screening memorandum for 75 active standalone software, technology and digital intellectual-property assets. Twelve classification families are used only to organise related subject matter.

Standalone IP asset boundary

Each active project is presented as a separate intellectual-property asset with its own TA-IP identifier, commercial description, seller-side value references, rights perimeter, diligence scope and potential transaction pathway. Inclusion in the same classification family does not merge the assets or their ownership, provenance, dependencies, value or transferability.

Classification-family boundary

The 12 families are taxonomy and navigation categories only. A family is not itself an IP asset, does not carry an independent asking price, does not imply technical equivalence or common implementation, and does not require its member assets to be sold, licensed or transferred together.

Multi-asset transactions

Two or more standalone assets may be negotiated in one transaction, but only where the relevant documentation expressly identifies each included asset and defines the rights and transfer perimeter for each.

Active commercial register

The public schedule contains 75 active commercial assets. Repository existence alone does not automatically add a project to that register. Newly created repositories are added only after separate asset qualification and commercial metadata are established. Retired or withdrawn projects are excluded from active counts and totals.

Retired identifier continuity

TA-IP-001 Research Orchestrator is retired and its repository has been removed. Its identifier remains reserved for historical continuity and is not reused. TA-IP-012 Research Intelligence Fabric remains a separate active asset line.

No offer or commitment

This website does not constitute a binding offer, agreement, commitment, prospectus, securities offering or promise to sell, license or transfer any asset. Any transaction is subject to negotiation, diligence and definitive documentation.

Indicative information

Portfolio values, asking references, ranges, development-stage labels, commercial-potential classifications and descriptions are seller-side and indicative. They are not independent appraisals, fairness opinions, audited valuations or guarantees of revenue, profitability, market adoption or transaction value.

Recreation cost

Recreation-cost figures are seller-side estimates of the resources required to recreate comparable assets from scratch. They are cost-approach references only and are not market value, fair value, transaction price, audited cost or a guarantee that a third party would incur the same expenditure.

Buyer diligence

A prospective buyer, licensee, technology-transfer counterparty or strategic partner should conduct commercial, legal, intellectual-property, financial, operational, scientific where relevant, and technical due diligence for each asset included in a proposed transaction.

Repository visibility and source access

Repository presence does not imply that source code is publicly disclosed, that every repository is production-ready, or that unrestricted source-level access will be provided before a counterparty and diligence perimeter are established.

Third-party rights

Any third-party software, data, standards, services, models, libraries or other materials associated with an asset remain subject to their respective rights, licences and terms. Transferability must be assessed separately for each asset.

Public inquiry boundary

Any public GitHub issue used to initiate contact is a routing mechanism only. Confidential information, source code, trade secrets, personal data, credentials, non-public commercial terms and other sensitive material must not be posted there.

Currency

EUR is the authoritative presentation currency. USD figures are indicative conversions using the stated reference rate for convenience and are not separate transaction prices.
