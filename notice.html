This website is a commercial screening memorandum for 79 standalone software, technology and digital intellectual-property assets organised into 12 classification families.

Active portfolio boundary

The public schedule contains 79 active standalone IP asset lines. Each project is presented as its own asset. Retired predecessors are excluded from the active count. Stable TA-IP identifiers are not renumbered or reused.

Commercial-reference boundary

All 79 active assets currently have individual seller-side asking references and asset-level economic records. Aggregate portfolio figures are calculated from individual asset records; classification families carry no separate valuation.

Distinct valuation concepts

The website distinguishes seller asking price, replacement-cost baseline, market/comparable proxy, income/licensing proxy, probability-adjusted strategic value and preliminary triangulated economic reference. These measures answer different questions and must not be treated as interchangeable.

Current portfolio references

The current aggregate seller asking reference is EUR 14.075 million. The current recreation/replacement-cost baseline is EUR 60.150 million. The asset-level market/comparable proxy aggregates to EUR 67.967 million, the income/licensing proxy to EUR 80.368 million, the probability-adjusted strategic lens to EUR 76.839 million and the preliminary triangulated economic reference to EUR 71.598 million. These are seller-side analytical references, not certified market values.

Valuation reference architecture

The methodology is informed by WIPO IP valuation guidance and technology-transfer valuation materials, International Valuation Standards including IVS 210 Intangible Assets, the RICS professional standard on valuation of intellectual-property rights, OECD guidance on intangibles and DEMPE/value creation, IFRS IAS 38 Intangible Assets, ISO 56005:2020 IP-management guidance, EPO patent-management and valuation guidance, JPO intellectual-property valuation materials and USPTO IP valuation/commercialization resources.

External market evidence

The current model includes a primary-source transaction evidence register spanning all eight portfolio sectors. These transactions are used to calibrate sector demand and evidentiary confidence. They are not presumed to be direct comparables for portfolio assets. Observed transaction values or revenue multiples must not be copied directly into an asset valuation without a subject-asset comparability analysis covering rights, maturity, scale, revenue, growth, useful life, geography, transaction date and transaction structure.

Illustrative mature-company ratios

A selected six-transaction subset with disclosed revenue produces rough deal-value/revenue ratios from approximately 4.88x to 24.98x, with a median around 13.05x. The sample mixes enterprise value and equity value, uses different revenue periods and consists of mature operating software companies. It is evidence of strategic software transaction economics, not a valuation multiple for early-stage or pre-revenue portfolio assets.

Replacement-cost completeness

The EUR 60.150 million replacement-cost figure is a current cost-approach baseline. It is not yet represented as a fully componentized all-in replacement-cost audit. Architecture, research, QA/validation, security, deployment, documentation, data/model work, productization, compliance, IP/licence qualification and programme-management effort must be reviewed asset by asset. No automatic uplift is applied and double counting is prohibited.

Probability model

The current probability-adjusted strategic model uses explicit assumptions for technical completion, commercialization probability, time/risk and a rights/transfer diligence reserve. These assumptions are transparent internal modelling inputs, not institution-prescribed probabilities, and should be replaced by stronger asset-specific evidence as diligence progresses.

Model-status boundary

References to WIPO, IVSC, RICS, OECD, IFRS, ISO, EPO, JPO and USPTO describe standards, principles and institutional guidance used to inform the framework. None of those organisations reviewed the repositories, certified the applied model, endorsed a value, issued a formal appraisal or approved a transaction price.

Retired predecessor continuity

TA-IP-001 Research Orchestrator has been retired after its underlying repository was removed. Its identifier remains reserved for historical continuity and is not an active commercial asset. TA-IP-012 Research Intelligence Fabric is the active successor line.

Classification-family boundary

The 12 families are navigation and classification categories only. A family is not an IP asset, has no separate ownership or asking reference, and does not merge, bundle or replace the individual identity of member assets.

No offer or commitment

This website does not constitute a binding offer, agreement, commitment, prospectus, securities offering or promise to sell, license or transfer any asset. Any transaction is subject to negotiation, diligence and definitive documentation.

Indicative information

Asking references, development-stage labels, commercial-potential classifications, replacement-cost estimates, market/income proxies and strategic/economic references are seller-side and indicative. They are not independent appraisals, fairness opinions, audited valuations or guarantees of revenue, profitability, market adoption or transaction value.

Buyer-specific valuation

A transaction-stage valuation may materially differ once reliable comparable transactions, royalty rates, revenues, margins, remaining development costs, useful lives, discount rates, real-options inputs, regulatory requirements, integration synergies and buyer-specific strategic benefits are available.

Buyer diligence

A prospective buyer, licensee, technology-transfer counterparty or strategic partner should conduct its own commercial, legal, intellectual-property, financial, operational, scientific where relevant, and technical due diligence before entering into a transaction.

Repository visibility and source access

Repository presence helps define the project universe but does not itself establish production readiness, public source disclosure or unrestricted diligence access.

Third-party rights

Any third-party software, data, standards, services, models, libraries or other materials associated with an asset remain subject to their respective rights, licences and terms. Transferability must be assessed at asset level.

Public inquiry boundary

Any public GitHub issue used to initiate contact is a routing mechanism only. Confidential information, source code, trade secrets, personal data, credentials, non-public commercial terms and other sensitive material must not be posted there.

Currency

EUR is the authoritative presentation currency. USD figures are indicative conversions using the stated reference rate and are not separate transaction prices.

Possible uses and counterparties

Application examples, potential buyer categories and suggested benefits are commercial hypotheses based on public asset descriptions and family classifications. They do not establish implemented features, customer adoption, revenue or measured outcomes.

Confidential implementation boundary

The site publishes commercial summaries and seller-side valuation references. It is not intended to distribute the underlying assets' source code, implementation designs, proprietary methods or restricted supporting material. A website confidentiality notice does not create a confidential access channel or substitute for separately agreed terms.

External support and partnership routes

EEN, the European IP Helpdesk and WIPO resources are provided for general orientation. No listing, formal partnership, eligibility decision, institutional endorsement or certification is represented.

Delivery and service scope

Possible package categories and diligence questions describe topics for negotiation. They are not a warranty of completeness. Operating services, support, hosting and maintenance require explicit agreement if included.
