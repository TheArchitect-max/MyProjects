This website is a commercial screening memorandum for 75 active software, technology and digital intellectual-property assets organised into 12 commercial product families.

Active portfolio boundary

The public schedule contains 75 active commercial asset lines. Retired or withdrawn predecessors are excluded from active counts, asking references, portfolio ranges and recreation-cost totals. Stable TA-IP identifiers are not renumbered or reused.

Retired predecessor continuity

TA-IP-001 Research Orchestrator has been retired after its underlying repository was removed. Its identifier remains reserved for historical continuity and is not an active commercial asset. TA-IP-012 Research Intelligence Fabric is the active successor line.

Product-family boundary

The 12 product families are a commercial navigation and rationalisation layer intended to make related capabilities easier to evaluate. Family membership does not mean that separate assets are technically identical, interchangeable, automatically bundled, or transferred together.

No offer or commitment

This website does not constitute a binding offer, agreement, commitment, prospectus, securities offering or promise to sell, license or transfer any asset. Any transaction is subject to negotiation, diligence and definitive documentation.

Indicative information

Portfolio values, asking references, ranges, development-stage labels, commercial-potential classifications and descriptions are seller-side and indicative. They are not independent appraisals, fairness opinions, audited valuations or guarantees of revenue, profitability, market adoption or transaction value.

Recreation cost

Recreation-cost figures are seller-side estimates of the resources required to recreate comparable assets from scratch. They are cost-approach references only and are not market value, fair value, transaction price, audited cost or a guarantee that a third party would incur the same expenditure.

Buyer diligence

A prospective buyer, licensee, technology-transfer counterparty or strategic partner should conduct its own commercial, legal, intellectual-property, financial, operational, scientific where relevant, and technical due diligence before entering into a transaction.

Repository visibility and source access

Repository presence is used to help define the active portfolio boundary. It does not imply that source code is publicly disclosed, that every repository is production-ready, or that unrestricted source-level access will be provided before a counterparty and diligence perimeter are established.

Third-party rights

Any third-party software, data, standards, services, models, libraries or other materials associated with an asset remain subject to their respective rights, licences and terms. Transferability must be assessed at asset level.

Public inquiry boundary

Any public GitHub issue used to initiate contact is a routing mechanism only. Confidential information, source code, trade secrets, personal data, credentials, non-public commercial terms and other sensitive material must not be posted there.

Currency

EUR is the authoritative presentation currency. USD figures are indicative conversions using the stated reference rate for convenience and are not separate transaction prices.
