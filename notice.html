This website is a commercial Information Memorandum intended to support initial discussions concerning a portfolio of software and digital intellectual-property assets.

Active portfolio boundary

The public schedule contains only active commercial asset lines supported by repositories currently present in the owner account at the latest reconciliation. Withdrawn or missing repository lines are removed from the active schedule; stable TA-IP identifiers are not renumbered or reused.

No offer or commitment

This memorandum does not constitute a binding offer, agreement, commitment, prospectus, securities offering or promise to sell, license or transfer any asset. Any transaction is subject to negotiation and definitive documentation.

Indicative information

Portfolio values, asking references, ranges, development-stage labels and commercial descriptions are indicative and may change. They are not independent appraisals, fairness opinions, audited valuations or guarantees of revenue, profitability, market adoption or transaction value.

Recreation cost

Recreation-cost figures are seller-side estimates of the resources required to recreate comparable assets from scratch. They are cost-approach references only and are not market value, fair value, transaction price, audited cost or a guarantee that a third party would incur the same expenditure.

Buyer diligence

A prospective buyer, licensee or partner should conduct its own commercial, legal, intellectual-property, financial, operational and technical due diligence before entering into a transaction.

Third-party rights

Any third-party software, data, standards, services, models or other materials associated with an asset remain subject to their respective rights, licences and terms.

Currency

EUR is the authoritative presentation currency. USD figures are indicative conversions using the stated reference rate for convenience and are not separate transaction prices.
