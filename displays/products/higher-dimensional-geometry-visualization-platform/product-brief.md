## Current technology and purpose

A proprietary research tool for constructing and investigating 4D geometry with source-attributed projections, sections, tesseract topology and browser demonstrations.

## Source-documentation basis

The README records accepted and merged P3 R11 with exact-source CI evidence, bounded independent reference comparisons and browser acceptance. That evidence applies to specified mathematical and software behaviours, not to perceptual or physical discovery claims.

## Demonstrated scope

- Native 4D topology and geometry with repeatable representations
- Multiple bounded projection/section and dual-view methods
- Offline browser demonstrations and test-recorded comparison procedures

## Not established

- Physical observation of a fourth spatial dimension
- Arbitrary n-dimensional projection and complete perceptual validation
- Production release engineering and unbounded mathematical guarantees

## Qualification and rights boundaries

The project visualizes mathematically specified extra degrees of freedom. It does not prove that physical extra dimensions exist or that humans can literally see 4D space. Source-specific software tests do not establish independent market value, operational readiness, all third-party rights, or terms of ownership transfer.
