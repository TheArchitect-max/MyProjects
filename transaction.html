Acquisition & licensing

Start with
a specific project.

Use the project reference and a general description of your interest. The initial contact channel is public; confidential diligence and transaction terms require a separately agreed channel.

A defined process.

  1. Identify. Select the project and intended use.
  2. Qualify. Agree the actual work product, development boundary and evidence needed.
  3. Protect the discussion. Establish an appropriate confidential channel and disclosure terms.
  4. Evaluate. Review rights, dependencies, reproducibility, operating costs and project-specific commercial inputs.
  5. Agree. Negotiate consideration, included rights, delivery, acceptance and any support obligations explicitly.

Pricing after qualification.

The current re-evaluation assigns no new asking prices. Previous references, where available, are historical and not reconfirmed. Acquisition, exclusive or non-exclusive licensing and technology transfer each require their own rights and pricing analysis.

No ongoing services or support are included by implication. Delivery scope and responsibilities must be agreed explicitly.
