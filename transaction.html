Transaction overview

Flexible scope.
Defined process.

The portfolio can support a whole-portfolio transaction, selected asset acquisitions, licensing structures or strategic partnerships. Detailed diligence follows only after the transaction perimeter and confidentiality framework are defined.

Potential structures

Four principal routes to a transaction.

No structure is implied by this memorandum. The appropriate perimeter depends on buyer objectives and negotiated rights.

01

Whole-portfolio acquisition

Acquisition of the selected active portfolio as a single strategic asset package.

02

Selected asset acquisition

Purchase of one or more individual assets or thematic clusters.

03

Exclusive / non-exclusive licensing

Rights structured by market, geography, field of use, exclusivity or term.

04

Strategic partnership

Commercialisation or development cooperation without immediate full transfer.

Indicative process

Commercial discussion first, diligence second.

The public IM is intentionally non-technical. Detailed information is appropriate only once there is a defined counterparty, transaction perimeter and confidentiality framework.

Transaction boundary. This Information Memorandum is an initial commercial overview only. No asset is represented as sold, licensed or transferred unless and until definitive documentation is executed.
