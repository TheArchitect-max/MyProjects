Transaction overview

Flexible scope.
Controlled diligence.

The portfolio can support a whole-portfolio transaction, selected asset acquisitions, licensing structures or strategic partnerships. Transaction-stage diligence should be proportionate to the rights and assets under consideration.

Potential structures

Four principal routes to a transaction.

No structure is implied by this memorandum. The appropriate perimeter depends on buyer objectives and negotiated rights.

01

Whole-portfolio acquisition

Acquisition of the selected portfolio as a single strategic asset package.

02

Selected asset acquisition

Purchase of one or more products or thematic clusters.

03

Exclusive / non-exclusive licensing

Rights structured by market, geography, field of use or term.

04

Strategic partnership

Commercialisation or development cooperation without immediate full transfer.

Indicative process

Commercial discussion first, diligence second.

The public IM is intentionally non-technical. Detailed diligence is appropriate only once there is a defined buyer, transaction perimeter and confidentiality framework.

1

Initial indication

Buyer identifies the desired portfolio scope, strategic rationale and preferred transaction structure.

2

Commercial alignment

Parties discuss valuation expectations, exclusivity, timing and principal terms.

3

Controlled diligence

Relevant technical, legal, IP and operational information is disclosed in proportion to the proposed transaction.

4

Definitive documentation

Rights, consideration, transition obligations, warranties and exclusions are documented.

5

Closing

Transfer or licensing occurs under the agreed perimeter and completion conditions.

6

Transition

Any agreed documentation, handover or limited transition support is delivered.

Transaction boundary. This Information Memorandum is an initial commercial overview only. No asset is represented as sold, licensed, certified or transferred unless and until definitive documentation is executed.
