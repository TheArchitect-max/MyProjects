Professional engagement

Identify the asset.
Then diligence it.

A serious discussion starts with a named standalone TA-IP asset and a defined transaction objective. Classification families can help discovery, but they are not transaction units.

Potential structures

Four principal transaction routes.

Every route is defined at asset level. Multiple projects may be negotiated together only when each included asset is expressly identified.

01

Single-asset acquisition

Purchase of one defined standalone software or technology IP asset.

02

Multi-asset acquisition

Purchase of two or more separately identified standalone assets under one negotiated transaction.

03

Exclusive / non-exclusive licensing

Rights structured for a defined asset by field of use, market, geography, exclusivity, term or other negotiated restrictions.

04

Technology transfer / strategic integration

A negotiated asset-specific transfer or integration perimeter without requiring acquisition of unrelated projects.

Indicative process

A five-gate route from first contact to definitive agreement.

The process protects sensitive information while preserving a clean asset-level chain from commercial interest to transfer.

01

Identify

Name the exact project and TA-IP reference, or list every asset if more than one is being considered.

02

Qualify

Define acquisition, licensing, technology transfer, strategic integration or another commercial structure.

03

Confidentiality

Agree counterparty identity, communication channel, disclosure perimeter and confidentiality framework.

04

Diligence

Review technical state, provenance, rights, dependencies, evidence, build material and transfer limitations for each named asset.

05

Terms & closing

Negotiate consideration, rights, representations, transfer mechanics and definitive documentation asset by asset.
