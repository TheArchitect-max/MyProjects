## Method

The review triangulates three valuation approaches. WIPO's cost method supports recreation/replacement cost as a useful baseline for internally developed software and early-stage technology where income forecasts and comparables are weak. Market evidence from Acquire.com, Software Equity Group, SaaS Capital and Flippa shows that operating software businesses are priced primarily from verified profit/ARR, growth, retention, strategic fit and transferability. Because verified portfolio-level ARR, profit, customer count and retention are not established, an income multiple is not applied to the current asset portfolio. Instead, recreation cost is discounted by evidence-bounded maturity, commercial potential and route-to-market, then checked against digital-asset liquidity constraints.

Strategic factors: Software-Validated 30%, Verified Prototype 16%, Research 7%; potential modifiers Very High 1.20, High 1.00, Moderate 0.85, Specialist 0.70; route modifiers 0.85–1.10. Negotiation bands are 80%–125% of the strategic point estimate. Liquidity references use lower maturity factors (12%, 6%, 2.5%). Values are rounded to €5,000.

## Evidence boundaries

Repository existence, private visibility and first-party proprietary posture are evidence about the observed repository state. They are not independent legal chain-of-title, patent/FTO, trademark or tax certification. Recreation cost is not market value. Strategic asking references are seller-side targets calibrated to current evidence and market conditions; a specific buyer may pay more for strategic fit or less for integration, diligence, concentration, regulatory, third-party-rights, deployment or commercialization risk. Any material new revenue, customer, retention, deployment, external benchmark, certification, patent, license or transaction evidence should trigger a new review.
